# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Orvulix welcomes contributors and users regardless of experience, background, identity, or location. We aim to provide a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and code reviews.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Help make contributions accessible and welcoming.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, trolling, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact or inappropriate conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the Orvulix repository, issues, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **founder@orvulix.io.vn** with relevant context. The project maintainer will review reports, seek additional information where necessary, and take proportionate action, including warnings, content removal, or restricting participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules fairly and to follow the same standards as contributors.
